Ultimate Rowing Series

A new multi-discipline regatta series for teams of four

About the Series

The Ultimate Rowing Series brings together the full spectrum of rowing competition into one unified series. Crews of four will compete across multiple formats, testing skill, adaptability, and teamwork.

Disciplines

Fixed Seat

Traditional power and endurance racing.

Coastal

Open water racing in challenging conditions.

Beach Sprint

Fast-paced racing from sand to sea and back.

Flatwater

Classic regatta racing on calm courses.

Format

Teams of four compete across all disciplines, earning points at each event. The overall series winner will be crowned the most complete rowing crew.
